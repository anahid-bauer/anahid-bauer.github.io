<h1>Anahid Bauer</h1>
<p class="subtitle">Associate Professor of Digital Economics<br>Institut Mines-Télécom Business School · <a href="https://www.litem.univ-evry.fr/accueil.html" target="\_blank" rel="noopener">LITEM</a></p>

<p>I am an applied economist working at the intersection of labor economics and industrial organization. My research studies how digital technologies (e-commerce, recommender algorithms and artificial intelligence) reshape labor markets, firms and the information people consume.</p>

<p>I received my PhD in Economics from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in 2022.</p>
