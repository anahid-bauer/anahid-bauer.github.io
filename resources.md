<h1>Resources</h1>

<p class="intro">Guidelines for the group activities I designed for my courses. You are welcome to reuse and adapt them with credit (<a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CC BY 4.0</a>).</p>

<h2>Data and AI</h2>

<div class="paper">
  <span class="title">From raw data to a business recommendation</span>
  <span class="meta">Introduction to Programming for Managers · Master (M1) · with Mariano Spivak</span>
  <span class="desc">Over three days, teams load, clean, merge and visualise a real business dataset in Python, and present their findings to a non-technical client.</span>
  <span class="links-inline"><a href="/assets/teaching/Intro_Programming_Group_Project.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Guidelines (PDF)</a></span>

<div class="paper">
  <span class="title">A defensible statistical answer to a business question</span>
  <span class="meta">Introduction to Data Science · Master (M1)</span>
  <span class="desc">A nine-session project built through weekly homework: descriptive statistics, hypothesis testing and regression, ending with an honest discussion of what the analysis can and cannot say about causality.</span>
  <span class="links-inline"><a href="/assets/teaching/Intro_Data_Science_Group_Project.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Guidelines (PDF)</a></span>
</div>

<div class="paper">
  <span class="title">An AI advisory brief for a manager</span>
  <span class="meta">Artificial Intelligence and Data Science · Master (M1)</span>
